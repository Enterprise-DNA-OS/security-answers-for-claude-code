## Candidate comparison

Scores are bill, data shape, buyer fit, search, buildability, import, each out of five. These are selection judgments, not measured demand statistics.

| Candidate | Scores | Total | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1up | 2, 5, 2, 3, 5, 4 | 21 | Public Plus monthly price annualises above five figures; CSV export is documented |
| HyperComply | 2, 5, 2, 3, 4, 3 | 19 | Vendor marketplace has enterprise annual contracts; no current direct public price verified and export mapping less clear |
| Conveyor | 1, 5, 2, 3, 4, 3 | 18 | Historical entry price is below the bill threshold; current direct pricing does not establish a qualifying annual bill |

1up Plus lists $900 per month with six questionnaire automations. Twelve months at that monthly price is $10,800. This is a public list-price scenario, not a verified NZ or AU customer's invoice. No currency conversion or implementation charge is assumed. The site also offers annual billing discounts; the scenario deliberately uses the displayed monthly option. No confirmed local customer use was found in the available customer and CRM files. Live CRM access was not verified. Search results establish alternative comparisons exist, not search volume. These limits keep buyer fit at two and demand at three.
